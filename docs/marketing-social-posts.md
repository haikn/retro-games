## Pre-launch

**Dev log (X/Bluesky/Mastodon)**
> Building a phone-LCD style game collection. Every "pixel" is a 5x5 block on a hidden grid, drawn from a 1-bit buffer. #pixelart #gamedev

**Teaser (Instagram/TikTok caption)**
> POV: your first phone, 2002. Something is coming. Waitlist in bio.

**Behind the scenes (thread)**
> 1/ The sounds in LCD Arcade are pure square waves, like old phone beepers.
> 2/ We synthesize them in code, no samples.
> 3/ Same idea for the font: Silkscreen, a pixel typeface that only covers Western letters, so every menu label is measured against its slot.
> Waitlist: [link]

**Countdown (all platforms)**
> One week to go. Four classic games, no ads, no sign-up. Be first: [link]
